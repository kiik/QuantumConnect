## Bomb settings

`serena#bomb=N`: a ready bomb is worth N% of the pattern table's value, so the bot keeps it for a
better moment. `serena#bombLine=Q`: bombs only next to a line with Q or more quarks of one player.
Late moves were counted at the old 100 ms limit.

## Why the live bot loses (33 losses of Tank-05-1101 to the top six, 15x30)
26 of the 33 end with the opponent's bomb as the winning move. We bomb as soon as the bomb is ready (every
20 plies); the top bots hold theirs and win with it. In about 17 games we bombed 4 to 8 plies before the end
while the opponent's bomb was ready, and a 1 s search sees a forced loss right after that bomb. On our last
six moves of each game (198 positions), the 40 ms move matched the 1 s move in 72%, at a mean depth of 4.10
against 5.51.
